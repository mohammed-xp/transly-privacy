Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Transly AI ("the app", "we", "us") is a translation app developed by Mohammed Adil, an independent developer based in the United Arab Emirates. This policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information

The app uses Firebase Authentication. When you continue as a guest, an anonymous account identifier is created for you. If you create or link an account, we receive your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive the name and profile picture associated with your Google account.

Text you translate online

When you use online translation, the text you enter is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which sends it to Google's Gemini API to generate the translation.

Translation history

Your translations are saved on our servers and linked to your account, so you can view them and sync them across your devices.

Usage and plan information

We record how many characters you translate and which plan you are on, so we can apply daily and plan-based usage limits.

Camera and photos

If you use camera or image translation, the app uses the image only to extract the text you want to translate. The image, or the text extracted from it, may be processed on your device or sent to our server and Google's Gemini API. We do not store your images. The app accesses your camera and photos only when you choose to use this feature and grant permission.

Microphone and voice

If you use voice translation, the app uses audio only to convert your speech into text, and only while voice input is active. Your speech may be processed on your device, by your device's speech recognition service, or sent to our server and Google's Gemini API. We do not store audio recordings. The app accesses your microphone only when you choose to use this feature and grant permission.

Technical information

Our server automatically records technical data such as IP address, device type, app version, request times, and error logs. We use this to keep the service secure and to fix problems.

Offline translation

Offline translation uses Google ML Kit and runs entirely on your device. Text you translate offline is not sent to us or to Google. ML Kit may download language models and send anonymous performance and usage metrics to Google.

2. How we use information

  • To provide translations and sync your translation history.
  • To create and manage your account.
  • To apply usage limits and manage plans and subscriptions.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix bugs.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information. The app does not show ads, and we do not use your data for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites.

3. Service providers

We use the following providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase Authentication: sign-in and account management.
  • Google Gemini API: online translation, including text extracted from images and speech.
  • Google ML Kit: on-device offline translation.
  • Microsoft Azure: hosting for our server and database.
  • Google Play: subscription payment processing, if you make a purchase.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy, Gemini API Terms, Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Payments

If paid plans are offered and you purchase a subscription, payment is processed by Google Play. We receive your subscription status. We never receive your payment card details.

5. Data retention

  • Account information, translation history, and usage records are kept while your account is active, until you delete your account.
  • Technical logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.
  • If you use the app as a guest and uninstall it without linking an account, you may lose access to your guest data.

6. Delete your account and data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: open Settings → Delete account.
  • By email: send a request from the email address linked to your account to m7md20039579@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Transly AI account".

When your account is deleted, we delete your account information, translation history, and usage records within 30 days. We may keep limited information longer only where required by law, or where needed to resolve disputes or prevent abuse.

7. Security

All communication between the app and our server uses encrypted HTTPS connections, and access to our systems is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children's privacy

Transly AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located in countries other than your own. Your information may be processed in those countries, and we take steps to protect it in line with this policy.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app.

النصوص التي تترجمها عبر الإنترنت

عند استخدام الترجمة عبر الإنترنت، يُرسَل النص الذي تُدخله عبر اتصال مشفّر إلى خادمنا، الذي يرسله بدوره إلى واجهة Gemini API من Google لإنتاج الترجمة.

سجل الترجمات

تُحفَظ ترجماتك على خوادمنا وترتبط بحسابك، حتى تتمكن من عرضها ومزامنتها بين أجهزتك.

يسجّل خادمنا تلقائيًا بيانات تقنية مثل عنوان IP ونوع الجهاز وإصدار التطبيق وأوقات الطلبات وسجلات الأخطاء. نستخدم هذه البيانات للحفاظ على أمان الخدمة وإصلاح المشكلات.

الترجمة دون اتصال بالإنترنت
